Choosing the right template is crucial for making a strong first impression while passing Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). All Ella templates are designed by HR professionals and tested for ATS compatibility.

## What Makes Our Templates ATS-Friendly?

All templates feature:
- ✅ Standard section headings (ATS recognizes "Experience", "Education", etc.)
- ✅ Simple, clean formatting (no complex graphics or tables)
- ✅ Machine-readable fonts
- ✅ Proper hierarchy and structure
- ✅ Keywords optimization support

## How to Switch Templates

1. Open your CV in the editor
2. Click "Change Template" in the top menu
3. Preview different options in real-time
4. Select your preferred design
5. Your content automatically adapts to the new layout

**No data loss!** Switching templates never affects your CV content.

## Template Customization

Premium users can customize:
- Colors and fonts (while maintaining ATS compatibility)
- Section order and visibility
- Spacing and margins
- Header styles
