Ella's AI writing assistant is your personal career coach that optimizes every section of your CV for maximum impact.

## How AI Analysis Works

After you [upload or create your CV](/help/getting-started-with-ella), Ella's AI evaluates:

- **Content Quality**: Flags vague descriptions and suggests quantifiable improvements with action verbs
- **ATS Optimization**: Identifies missing industry keywords and ensures standard formatting
- **Career Alignment**: Recommends suitable job titles and highlights skills gaps

## AI Suggestion Examples

**Professional Summary Enhancement**  
Before: "Experienced manager with good leadership skills"  
After: "Results-driven Operations Manager with 8+ years leading teams of 15+, achieving 25% efficiency improvements"

**Achievement Quantification**  
Before: "Improved sales performance"  
After: "Increased quarterly sales by 34% ($2.5M) through strategic client relationship management"

**Industry Keywords**  
Software Engineer → "Agile, CI/CD, microservices, cloud architecture"  
Marketing Manager → "SEO, conversion optimization, A/B testing, ROI"

**Free:** Basic suggestions, grammar checks, simple keywords

## Best Practices

**Do:** Review each suggestion, maintain your authentic voice, re-analyze after edits  
**Don't:** Accept all blindly, over-optimize with keywords, remove personal achievements
