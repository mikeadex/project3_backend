# Downloading and Exporting Your CV: Format Guide

**Category:** Help  
**Tags:** export, download, pdf, docx, formats  
**Excerpt:** Learn how to download your CV in different formats (PDF, DOCX, TXT) and when to use each format for maximum compatibility.

---

Once you've created your CV with Ella, export it in multiple formats for different application requirements.

## Available Export Formats

### PDF (Recommended)

**Best for:** Most job applications, email submissions

- Maintains precise layout and design
- Works on all devices
- Cannot be accidentally edited
- Professional appearance guaranteed

### DOCX (Microsoft Word)

**Best for:** Recruiters requesting editable files, recruitment agencies

- Fully editable by recipients
- ATS compatible
- Common in recruitment agencies

### TXT (Plain Text)

**Best for:** Strict ATS systems, web form applications

- 100% ATS-readable
- No formatting issues
- Maximum compatibility

## How to Download

1. Open your CV in editor or preview
2. Click "Download" or "Export" button
3. Select preferred format
4. Choose save location
5. Download instantly!

**All formats maintain your content** – only presentation changes.

## Best Practices

### File Naming

Use professional names:

- ✅ `FirstName_LastName_CV_2025.pdf`
- ✅ `John_Smith_Resume_Software_Engineer.pdf`
- ❌ `my_cv_final_FINAL_v3.pdf`

### Format Selection

- **PDF:** Default for most applications
- **DOCX:** When explicitly requested
- **TXT:** Government or strict ATS systems

## Common Questions

**Q: Why does PDF look different from preview?**  
A: Ensure latest browser. Refresh and retry.

**Q: Can recruiters edit my PDF?**  
A: No, PDFs are read-only. Use DOCX for editable versions.

**Q: Most ATS-friendly format?**  
A: All formats are optimized, but TXT has highest compatibility.
