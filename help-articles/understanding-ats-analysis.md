Ella's AI-powered analysis reveals how well your CV performs with Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) used by 90% of companies.

## What Is ATS Analysis?

After [uploading your CV](/help/getting-started-with-ella), click "Analyze CV" to evaluate:

- **ATS Score (0-100)**: Likelihood of passing automated screening
- **Keyword Optimization**: Industry-specific terms for your field
- **Formatting Issues**: ATS-unfriendly elements
- **Content Quality**: Achievement impact and descriptions
- **Role Suggestions**: Suitable job titles based on experience

## Score Interpretation

**80-100 (Excellent):** Highly optimized. Focus on keyword density and achievements.  
**60-79 (Good):** Solid foundation. Address formatting and add industry keywords.  
**40-59 (Needs Work):** Significant optimization needed. Review structure and keywords.  
**Below 40 (Critical):** Major issues. Use [AI improvement features](/help/ai-cv-improvements).

## Analysis Components

### Keyword Analysis

Identifies missing industry terms and suggests relevant additions.

### Formatting Check

Flags ATS-unfriendly elements:

- Complex tables/graphics
- Unusual section headings
- Non-standard fonts
- Images in critical areas

### Content Assessment

Evaluates action verbs, quantifiable achievements, date consistency, and professional summary strength.

### Role Suggestions

Recommends suitable job titles you qualify for.

## Improvement Steps

1. **Add Keywords**: Use industry terms naturally
2. **Quantify Achievements**: Include numbers and metrics
3. **Fix Formatting**: Choose [ATS-friendly templates](/help/choosing-cv-templates)
4. **Apply AI Suggestions**: Use [improvement tools](/help/ai-cv-improvements)
5. **Re-analyze**: Check score after edits
